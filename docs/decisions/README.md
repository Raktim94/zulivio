# Decision records

Short, numbered records of decisions that are expensive to reverse or
surprising to a reader who wasn't there — schema shapes, backward-compat
constraints, what was reused versus built, and what was deliberately left
out.

Not every change needs one. Add a record when a future maintainer would
otherwise have to reconstruct the reasoning from the diff.
